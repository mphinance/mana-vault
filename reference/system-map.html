System Map & Agent-Team Ownership

Mana Vault treats a Magic: The Gathering collection like a stock portfolio. It's small enough for one person to hold in their head — but the moment you want an agent team, you need clean, non-overlapping slices. This maps every surface into 7 ownership domains, each a candidate team with a clear mandate and the exact scripts, views, routes and data files it owns. Below the teams: the shared foundation everything stands on, and the cross-domain seams where a change ripples (the coordination-heavy spots — including one open gap). Click any team to expand. Search to find who owns a file. Companion: agent-teams matrix.
